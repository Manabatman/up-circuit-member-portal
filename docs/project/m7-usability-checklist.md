# M7 member usability checklist

Run with 2–3 real members **without explaining where things are**. Observe where they struggle.

## Tasks

1. Find the Official Academic Drive (renewed member account).
2. Find the UP Circuit Constitution.
3. Find the renewal portal starting from the sidebar **Renew Membership** page.
4. From a logged-out browser, find the first-time renewal form on the login page.
5. Open **Divisions** and pick any division.
6. Confirm division pages show an honest empty state when no links exist.
7. Look for a request form on **Resources** — expect empty Requests copy, not fake links.

## Discovery tasks (phone)

Give a member the phone **without explaining where things are**. Ask:

1. "You need to find the Constitution. Show me how you'd do it."
2. "You need to renew your membership. Show me what you'd do."
3. "You need something from your division. What would you click?"

You are testing whether they **naturally discover** the path — not whether they can follow your intended flow.

Test on an **actual phone**, not only Chrome's device emulator.

## Pass criteria

- Each task completed in under ~60 seconds without help.
- No member clicks a broken or placeholder URL.
- NOT_RENEWED members see a clear renewal path; RENEWED members are not told to renew again.
- Discovery tasks succeed without hints.
